Withdrawals? Fixed order.

Roth IRA withdrawals come out in a fixed order. Regular contributions first — always tax-free, always penalty-free. Conversions next, first in, first out. Earnings last.

Each conversion starts its own five-year clock. The five-year rule applies per conversion. Convert in 2023, withdraw in 2025 — that's under five years. The 10% penalty applies.

The order matters. Withdraw $20,000 from a Roth holding $15,000 in contributions and $5,000 in earnings. The first $15,000 is yours. The next $5,000 is earnings — and can trigger the 10% penalty under fifty-nine and a half.

Save this before your first withdrawal.
