FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
Roth IRA withdrawals follow a fixed order (IRC §408A(d)(4)):

1. Regular contributions first — always tax-free.
2. Conversions on a first-in, first-out basis.
3. Earnings last.

The $20,000 example:
- Roth holds: $15,000 contributions + $5,000 earnings
- Withdraw $20,000
- First $15,000: no tax, no penalty
- Next $5,000: ordinary income + 10% penalty under 59½

Each conversion starts its own 5-year clock. Convert in 2023, withdraw in 2025 — under 5 years. 10% penalty applies.

Track it on Form 8606.
