FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
SECURE Act 2.0 §401 + IRC §401(a)(9): non-spouse beneficiaries must empty an inherited IRA by Dec 31 of the 10th year following the original owner's death.

For the Roth: tax-free on the way out — but the empty-by rule is hard. Miss it, 25% excise tax (10% if corrected).

Exempt: spouses, minor children, disabled, <10 years younger.

Two strategies:
1. Lump sum in year 9: max tax-free growth
2. Spread years 1-10: $500K Roth → $50K/yr, all tax-free

The Roth's tax-free nature is the killer advantage over a traditional IRA inheritance.

Talk to your CPA before year 1, not year 9.
