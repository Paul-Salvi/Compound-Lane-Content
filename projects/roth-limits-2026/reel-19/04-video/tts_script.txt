Inherited Roth? Ten years.

Most non-spouse beneficiaries must empty an inherited Roth IRA by the end of year ten. Withdrawals stay tax-free — but the empty-by rule is hard.

Strategy one: wait until year nine, then withdraw everything in one lump sum. Tax-free for the Roth. Captures the maximum tax-free growth window.

Strategy two: spread withdrawals over years one through ten, taking roughly 10% per year — a $500,000 Roth becomes $50,000 annual withdrawals, all tax-free.

Save this before you inherit one.
