FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
The 2026 Roth IRA limit is $7,500 ($8,600 at 50+). Contribute more, and the excess is hit with a 6% excise tax — every year the excess stays (IRC §4973).

The $1,000 example:
- Excess: $1,000
- Annual excise tax: $60
- 5-year cumulative: $300
- Plus earnings on the excess accrue their own 6%

Two fixes:
1. Recharacterize as a traditional IRA (if eligible).
2. Withdraw the excess + earnings before your tax-filing deadline.

File Form 5329 either way.

Save this before you max out.
