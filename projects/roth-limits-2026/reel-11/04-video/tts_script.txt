Over-contributed? Six percent a year.

Contribute more than $7,500 — the excess gets a 6% excise tax. Every year the excess stays. The tax is on the excess only.

The $1,000 example. You contribute $8,500 instead of $7,500 — $1,000 excess. The 6% tax: $60 a year. Five years: $300. Plus the earnings on the excess accrue their own 6%.

The fix: recharacterize the excess as a traditional IRA — if eligible. Or withdraw the excess plus earnings before your tax-filing deadline. File Form 5329 to report the tax, or claim the withdrawal exception.

Save this before you max out.
