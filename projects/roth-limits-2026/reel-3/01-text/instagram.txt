FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
A Roth IRA for a minor is a custodial account. The parent or guardian opens it, the teen owns it. Three numbers decide the 2026 contribution:

1. Earned income. W-2 wages, tips, or self-employment. Allowance and investment gains don't count.
2. The 2026 limit: $7,500. But the contribution can't exceed earned income. A 16-year-old with a $3,000 summer job can put in $3,000.
3. The year they turn 18. A custodial Roth converts to a regular Roth on that birthday. The 5-year clock for tax-free earnings withdrawal starts that day.

Open a custodial Roth before the first W-2. The earlier, the better.

Educational content, not financial advice.
