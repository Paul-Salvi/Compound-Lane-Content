FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
Married filing jointly gets a smaller window than single filers. The 2026 MFJ Roth phase-out runs $242,000 to $252,000 of MAGI — a $10,000 window, vs the single filer's $15,000 window.

Inside it, IRS Pub 590-A Worksheet 2-2 reduces your allowed contribution. Above $252,000, neither spouse can contribute directly. Zero.

The line that matters is joint MAGI, not household salary. AGI plus a few add-backs = MAGI for Roth purposes.

Above the ceiling? The spousal backdoor Roth is the workaround — each spouse opens a non-deductible traditional IRA, then converts. Pro-rata rule applies (separate reel).

Estimate joint MAGI before December 31. If you're inside the window, decide what each spouse puts in. If you're above, plan the backdoor for January.

Educational content, not financial advice.
