FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
The 2026 IRA contribution limit is the same for both account types: $7,500. The only difference between Roth and Traditional is when you pay tax. That's the whole decision.

Traditional: deduct now, pay at withdrawal. Roth: pay now, withdraw tax-free.

The rule: compare your marginal rate today to your expected retirement rate. If retirement rate is higher, Roth wins. If retirement rate is lower, Traditional wins.

Most young workers in the 12%–24% bracket lean Roth. High earners in the 32%–35% bracket who expect a lower retirement rate lean Traditional.

$7,500/yr × 30yr × 7% = ~$708k. Roth keeps the full $708k. Traditional keeps ~$538k after 24% retirement tax. The $170k gap is tax-free compounding.

Same limit, different timing. The decision is yours.

Educational content, not financial advice.
