Roth IRA contribution deadline for tax year 2025: April 15, 2026 (per IRC §219(f)(3) + Pub 590-A).

Where the Sept 15 myth comes from:
- Sept 15 = quarterly estimated tax payment deadline
- Sept 15 = certain business returns on extension
- Both are unrelated to IRAs

The actual rules:
1. April 15 of the year after
2. October 15 with Form 4868 extension
3. After Oct 15 = contribution barred
4. Late contribution = next year, not the year you meant

Lose the room: $7,500 of 2025 room expires on the calendar. Gone forever.

Set a reminder for April 15.
