Sept 15? Wrong deadline.

Roth IRA contributions for tax year 2025 are due April 15, 2026 — the same as your tax return. Sept 15 is for estimated tax payments and certain business returns. Not IRAs.

File an extension? Your IRA contribution deadline extends to October 15 of the year after. After October 15, the contribution is barred. The deadline is a hard cutoff.

Contribute $7,500 on October 16 and the IRS treats it as a 2027 contribution. You lose 12 months of compounding on $7,500 — and the 2026 room is gone.

Save this before you miss the room.
