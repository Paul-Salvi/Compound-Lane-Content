The 10% penalty, IRC §72(t):
- Applies to EARNINGS only
- On top of ordinary income tax
- Before age 59½

The 5 exceptions:
1. First-time home purchase — $10K lifetime
2. Qualified higher education
3. Medical > 7.5% of AGI
4. Health insurance while unemployed
5. Birth or adoption — $5K per child

The strategy:
- Max the Roth
- Stack 6-12 months in a brokerage for emergencies
- The penalty is what makes the tax-free growth possible

Contributions can come out any time, tax- and penalty-free.
