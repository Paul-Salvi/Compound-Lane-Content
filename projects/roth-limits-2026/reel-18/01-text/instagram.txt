FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
Roth IRA (per IRC §408A):
- Tax-free in
- Tax-free growth
- Tax-free out (qualified)
= 2 tax events avoided

HSA (per IRC §223):
- Tax-free in
- Tax-free growth
- Tax-free out (medical)
- FICA dodge on payroll contributions
= 3 tax events avoided + 7.65% bonus

After 65, the HSA's edge is gone. Non-medical withdrawals become ordinary income. The Roth has no age cutoff.

Account priority:
1. HSA (triple tax-free)
2. Roth IRA
3. Roth 401(k)
4. Traditional 401(k)

Save this before your next account decision.
