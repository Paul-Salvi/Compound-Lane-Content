Roth vs HSA. Triple tax-free.

The Roth is tax-free on the way in. Tax-free on the way out. Two tax events avoided. Growth is also tax-free.

The HSA is tax-free on the way in, tax-free on growth, and tax-free on the way out for medical. Three tax events avoided. Payroll contributions also dodge FICA — a 7.65% bonus the Roth can't match.

After sixty-five, the HSA loses its triple-tax edge. Non-medical withdrawals become ordinary income — like a traditional IRA. The Roth stays tax-free at any age, any reason. HSA first, Roth second.
