FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
The two 5-year clocks, IRC §408A(d):

Earnings clock (§408A(d)(2)):
- One clock, opened at first contribution
- Governs tax-free earnings withdrawals

Conversion clock (§408A(d)(4)):
- NEW clock per conversion
- 10% penalty if pulled before expiry, even after 59½

The trap: $50K converted in 2024. Withdraw $10K in 2026 at age 62. 10% penalty — clock doesn't expire until 2030.

The fix:
- Convert early in the year
- Wait until 59½ AND clock expired
- Stack conversions in low-income years
- Track on Form 8606 Part II
