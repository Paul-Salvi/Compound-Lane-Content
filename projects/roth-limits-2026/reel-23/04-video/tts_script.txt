Two clocks on every Roth.

Roth conversions start their own clock. The IRS tracks each conversion separately. Pull before it expires — and the penalty hits, even past the age threshold.

The original Roth IRA has another clock. It opened when you first contributed. It governs earnings withdrawals.

The fix: convert early. Gives you five full calendar years before the clock runs out. Wait to withdraw until both the age threshold and the clock are met.

Stack conversions. One large conversion in a low-income year beats many small ones. Save this before your next conversion.
