Roth doesn't bump SS.

Roth IRA withdrawals don't count toward the Social Security provisional income formula. Trad IRA withdrawals do. Up to eighty five percent of SS can become taxable.

Above thirty four K single, fifty percent of SS becomes taxable. Above forty four K, eighty five percent. Roth doesn't enter this.

The gap. Same $50,000 at sixty seven. Trad IRA: it adds to provisional income. Up to $42,000 of your SS becomes taxable. Roth: it doesn't count.
