FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
The rule, IRC §86:
- Provisional income = other income + 50% SS + tax-exempt interest
- Below $34K single: 0% SS taxable
- $34K-$44K: up to 50% SS taxable
- Above $44K: up to 85% SS taxable
- Roth withdrawals DON'T count. Trad IRA withdrawals DO.

The gap:
- Same $50K at 67
- Trad IRA: $50K adds to provisional. Up to $42K SS taxable. ~$9K extra tax.
- Roth: doesn't count. $0 extra tax on SS.

The strategy:
- Keep provisional income under $34K single / $44K MFJ
- Use Roth for early retirement spending
- Save Trad IRA for 70+ when RMDs force it out
