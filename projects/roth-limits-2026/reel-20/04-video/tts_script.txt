Same dollars, different account.

Put $7,500 a year into a Roth for thirty years at 7% real return. The taxable brokerage with the same dollars has two tax events: dividends annually, capital gains on sale. Long-term capital gains rate is 15%.

The brokerage wins on flexibility. No contribution limit, no income phaseout, no 5-year rule, no 10% penalty. Use it for the dollars above the Roth limit.

Roth ends at roughly $708,000, all tax-free. Brokerage ends at roughly $570,000 — and you keep every dollar. The gap is $138,000. Not millions.

Save this before you cap out the Roth.
