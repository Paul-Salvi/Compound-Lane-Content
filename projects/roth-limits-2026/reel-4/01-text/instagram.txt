FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
The 2026 single-filer Roth phase-out runs from $153,000 to $168,000 of modified adjusted gross income (MAGI). That's a $15,000 window. Inside it, IRS Pub 590-A Worksheet 2-2 reduces your allowed contribution. Above $168,000, you cannot contribute directly. Zero.

The line that matters is MAGI, not your salary. AGI plus a few add-backs = MAGI for Roth purposes.

Above the ceiling? The backdoor Roth is the workaround — non-deductible traditional IRA, then convert. Pro-rata rule applies (separate reel).

Estimate MAGI before December 31. If you're inside the window, decide what to put in. If you're above, plan for January.

Educational content, not financial advice.
