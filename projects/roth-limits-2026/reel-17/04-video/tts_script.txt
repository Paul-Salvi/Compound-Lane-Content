Form 8606 does the work.

Form 8606 Part One tracks non-deductible traditional IRA contributions. Part Two tracks Roth conversions. Without it, the IRS assumes every dollar in your Roth is pre-tax earnings.

File it every year you do a non-deductible contribution or a Roth conversion. Attach to your 1040. Skipped years can be amended with 1040-X.

Skip it for a $7,500 backdoor Roth — and thirty years later, when you withdraw $400,000, the IRS treats the entire amount as taxable. At 24% federal tax: $96,000 owed. The form costs nothing.

Save this before your next backdoor.
