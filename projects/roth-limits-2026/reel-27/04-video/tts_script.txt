Self-employed? Four accounts.

You have four retirement accounts. Three allow Roth. One is Roth-only. Funding order changes everything.

The four. Solo 401k. SEP IRA, pre-tax only. SIMPLE IRA. Roth IRA, $7,500. Solo 401k and SIMPLE have Roth options.

The funding order. First: Solo 401k employee deferral, $24,500, pre-tax or Roth. Second: Roth IRA, $7,500. Third: Solo 401k profit-sharing, up to the $72,000 cap.

Most self-employed only use SEP. They leave the Roth mega-backdoor on the table. Solo 401k has Roth, SEP doesn't. Skipping Solo 401k is a six-figure tax loss over thirty years. Save this before next quarter.
