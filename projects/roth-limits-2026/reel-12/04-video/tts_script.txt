The 401(k) Roth is bigger.

The 2026 Roth IRA limit is $7,500. The 2026 Roth 401(k) limit is $24,500. Same tax-free growth — $17,000 more room.

The Roth 401(k) has no income phaseout. The phaseout is IRA-only. High earners: the 401(k) Roth is your only direct route.

The mega backdoor: after-tax 401(k) plus in-plan conversions can push annual Roth savings above $70,000. A high earner with employer match can exceed $80,000 a year.
