emergency fund sizing: 5 things to know

emergency fund
how much?

1

Why Not 3–6 Months

The 3–6 month rule is a population average, not your number. 45% of Americans can't cover 3 months of expenses — flat for two years. Your target depends on job type, income stability, and dependents. One formula, four answers.

WARNING
45% of Americans can't cover 3 months of expenses (Fed SHED 2025, published 2026). 30% can't cover it by any means.
2

The Formula

Emergency fund = essential monthly expenses × target months. Essentials only: rent, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments, transportation. Not your income. Not your total spending. The cheapest number wins.

WRONG BASE

  • Uses gross income
  • Includes eating out + subscriptions
  • Inflates the target
vs

RIGHT BASE

  • Essentials only
  • Rent, food, insurance, transit
  • Smaller fund, same safety
3

Pick Your Months

FDIC baseline is 3–6 months. Dual income, both stable, low fixed costs → 3 months. Single stable income or dependents → 6 months. Self-employed, commission, or contract work → 9–12 months (industry consensus above FDIC baseline). Add 1–2 months if you have heavy fixed costs or a niche job.

Job typeTarget months
Dual income, both stable3
Single stable income6
Single income + dependents6–8
Self-employed / commission9–12
Variable income + heavy fixed9–12
4

Where to Park It

High-yield savings account, not checking, not investing. Today's top HYSAs pay 4.20–4.50% APY (up to 5.00% intro at some banks) — a real yield, FDIC-insured, liquid in 1 day. PCE inflation is 2.7% (Fed FOMC March 2026), so your fund actually grows above inflation while it sits.

CHECKING / INVESTING

  • Checking pays ~0%
  • Investing can drop 30%+ in a year
  • Both defeat the purpose
vs

HIGH-YIELD SAVINGS

  • 4.20–4.50% APY in 2026
  • FDIC-insured, 1-day liquidity
  • Beats 2.7% PCE inflation
5

Build It in 3 Steps

Step 1: Save one month of essentials first — your starter fund. Step 2: Automate weekly transfers to a separate HYSA. Step 3: Once the starter is hit, raise contributions until you reach your target. Don't invest the fund. Don't touch it for lifestyle.
