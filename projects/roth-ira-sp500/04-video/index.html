Roth IRA:
best deal.

1

Tax-free growth

You put in money you've already paid taxes on. It grows for decades, and you never owe a penny of tax on the growth. Not on the way in. Not on the way out.

ON THE WAY IN

  • You pay tax now
  • Money is post-tax
  • Growth tracked separately
vs

ON THE WAY OUT

  • No tax on withdrawal
  • No tax on dividends
  • Compounding stays whole
2

The Catch

But there's a cap. Seven thousand dollars a year — that's the limit for 2024. And it's earned income only — you can't deposit gifted money or returns from other investments.

$7,000 / YEAR CAP
Plus an income floor: you can only contribute what you earned this year. No 1099 income, no Roth contribution. Side gigs, salary, and self-employment count.
3

The Payoff

Max out your Roth for thirty years at seven percent. You retire with over a million dollars — tax free. That's the compounding engine no taxable account can match.

$1,000,000+
over 30 years, tax free
$7,000/year × 30 years @ 7% = $7,000 × (1.07³⁰ × 30 / 0.07) ≈ $1.05M
