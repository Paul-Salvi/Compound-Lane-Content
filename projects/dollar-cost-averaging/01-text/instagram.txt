FULL CAPTION (300-500 chars total — Compound Lane "study notes" format):
Dollar-cost averaging means $833 a month for 12 months to deploy $10,000 — not all at once. You buy fewer shares when prices are high, more when low, smoothing your average cost.

Vanguard and Schwab both tested rolling 12-month windows going back to the 1920s. Lump sum beat 12-month DCA about 68% of the time. Why? Markets rise more often than they fall — more time in = more compounding.

At 7%, $10k day-one → ~$10,700. DCA end-of-month → ~$10,327. The ~$373 gap is cash drag, not a fee.

The right way to DCA: automate paycheck investing on payday (no drag — money was never in cash). For windfalls, invest promptly or DCA 3–6 months max. Park waiting cash in yield.

Educational content, not financial advice.
