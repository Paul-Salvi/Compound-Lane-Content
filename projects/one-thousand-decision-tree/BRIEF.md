# BRIEF — $1,000 Decision Tree

> Confirmed intent for the `one-thousand-decision-tree` HyperFrames composition. Read this first; the rest of the project files (STORYBOARD, frame, composition) follow from it.

## Message

Explain **"I have $1,000 — what do I actually do with it?"** in ~90 seconds, so a retail investor who's just sat down with their first thousand dollars leaves the video knowing the **4-step decision tree** in order:

1. **Cash first** — keep $500–$1,000 in a high-yield savings account (4–5%, FDIC). The reason is optionality, not interest.
2. **Kill debt over 8%** — credit cards run 22–28%, BNPL runs 0–30%. Paying 24% is a guaranteed, tax-free, risk-free 24% return. You will never beat it.
3. **Roth IRA + S&P 500** — if you have earned income and you're under 50, a Roth IRA is the single best deal in personal finance. $7,000/year (2024), grows tax-free. Pick VOO, VTSAX, or FZROX for an expense ratio under 0.10%.
4. **Rest to brokerage** — the leftover $0–$500 goes into a regular taxable brokerage. Same S&P 500 fund, no tax break, but no contribution limit and no 59½ penalty. The flexible bucket.

The frame: **"A friend who works in finance sketched this on a napkin"** — informal, specific, quotable. Not textbook. The viewer walks away with an *order*, not a lecture.

## Audience

Retail investors, US, late-20s to mid-30s. Sitting on their first $1,000. Know the *term* "Roth IRA" and "S&P 500" but have never been told **which order** to do them in. Skim-style attention — the whiteboard reveal keeps the eye on the page, the VibeVoice/Paul narration keeps the ear. **Tag-a-friend shareable** ("@ your friend who still has $1,000 in checking").

Distribution: vertical short-form (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) — 1080×1920, 9:16.

## Audio

Two layers, mixed:

- **Voiceover (VO)**: 7 clips (header + 4 concepts + outro), TTS via local **VibeVoice 1.5B with the custom "Paul" voice** (per memory `vibevoice-paul-tts.md`). Drawn from `01-content/one-thousand-decision-tree.json` `concepts[i].body` for the concept beats and hand-edited for AUDIO_STYLE.md prosody. Conversational pace, em-dash breath, numbers-as-words.
- **SFX (paper-explainer profile)**: scribble on each concept appearance, chalk draw on underlines, highlight pop on yellow `.hl` keywords, card placement on each `compare-card` / `warning-box` / `flow-node`. Generated by `scripts/sound/emit-static-sfx.mjs` from `window.__soundSchedule` (auto-built by the inline script). The DCA-style static `<audio>` block (SFX as separate declarative cues) is **not** used here — the runtime schedule path is simpler and matches the 4-concept scope.
- **No music bed.** Quiet teaching style; the pen + voice is the whole show.

## Pointer style

**Pen tip, in the notebook's hand-drawn style.**

Source: inline SVG pen (rotated -45°, blue barrel, silver clip, drop-shadowed). Anchored at the **tip** so it lands on the word the voiceover is currently writing. The "realistic" here means: an actual pen shape, not a flat icon — drawn in the same ink-on-paper style as the rest of the page so it sits on the notebook, not pasted on.

Why SVG over a photo cutout: no media provider installed in this environment; a cutout PNG would clash with the hand-drawn page. The SVG keeps the visual language consistent and the per-word positioning is animatable via GSAP.

## Reveal style

**Flash preview + word-by-word reveal.** A 1.0s flash of the **finished** page opens the video, then fades out as the whiteboard reveal begins. Each section's elements (num-blob → h2 → lede → visual) reveal in turn, paced so per-element delay = sectionDuration / elementCount. The pen traverses the page as the words appear, the voiceover runs concurrently, and the page is fully written by the outro.

This is **the same reveal style as the DCA promo reel** — only the content is different.

## Customizations

- **Pen tip is the write point.** The pen SVG is positioned so the tip of the nib lands on the word the voiceover is currently writing. The barrel trails behind.
- **Words are wrapped in `<span class="w">` with deterministic per-word rotation** (a hash of the word's text → ±1.5°). This gives the hand-drawn "wobbly hand" feel without per-word randomness.
- **Sections reveal in the order they appear on the page** (top → bottom), but with a per-section `REVEAL` duration that matches the audio: 5s header + 4×~18-21s concepts + 12s outro. The build script's auto-defaults are a first-pass placeholder; re-measure with faster-whisper after the first VO regen.
- **No text overlays.** The page already has all the copy. Adding captions would duplicate it. The pen + voiceover carry the explanation.
- **Color rotation**: concept 1 ink-navy, concept 2 ink-maroon, concept 3 ink-green, concept 4 ink-body. Each `num-blob`/h2 picks up its color from the `b1..b4`/`c-maroon`/`c-green` class on the page. One ink per frame.
