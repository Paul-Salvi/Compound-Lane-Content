roth ira income limits (2026): 6 things to know

Roth IRA
income limits

1

The 2026 numbers

In 2026 you can contribute the full $7,500 to a Roth IRA ($8,600 at 50+ with catch-up). The 2026 401(k) elective deferral limit is $24,500 ($32,500 at 50+). The two caps are separate — you can max both if you have the earned income.

2

MAGI is the gate

Eligibility runs on Modified Adjusted Gross Income, not your salary. MAGI is your AGI plus add-backs — mainly foreign earned income exclusion, foreign housing amounts, and excluded income from Puerto Rico or American Samoa. For most people, MAGI equals AGI.

✳ tip —
MAGI ≈ AGI for most people. Pre-tax 401(k) contributions lower MAGI; a year-end bonus can push you over a threshold even if base salary is under it.
3

The phase-out ramp

The phase-out is a ramp, not a cliff. Below the floor you get the full amount; inside, your limit shrinks proportionally; at or above the top, you get zero. Single window: $153K to $168K. Joint window: $242K to $252K. Married-separate: $0 to $10K.

4

The phase-out math

The IRS formula is proportional: (top of range − your MAGI) ÷ range size × the limit. Single at $160,500: ($168,000 − $160,500) ÷ $15,000 × $7,500 = $3,750 allowed. Joint at $247,000: $5,000 ÷ $10,000 × $7,500 = $3,750.

5

Over the top? 3 options

Above $168K single or $252K joint, you can't contribute directly — but Roth-style saving isn't over. Backdoor Roth: contribute to a traditional IRA, convert to Roth. Max the 401(k) ($24,500, no income cap). Or use a taxable brokerage for overflow — no MAGI test, same low-cost funds.

Over-top paths

  • Backdoor Roth: tIRA → convert
  • Max 401(k): $24,500, no MAGI cap
  • Taxable brokerage: no MAGI test
vs

Watch-outs

  • Backdoor: pro-rata aggregates IRAs
  • Conversion taxable on pre-tax $
  • Taxable loses tax-free growth
6

Inside the window

The partial still compounds. At 7% for 30 years, $3,750/year grows to roughly $354,000 — half the ~$708,000 from a full $7,500 (hypothetical, constant return). The window is narrow: a $5,000 raise inside the $15K single window cuts a third of your allowed amount.

✳ source —
Single partial example: $3,750/yr × 30 yr at 7% → ~$354,000. Half the contribution, half the outcome. Estimate MAGI in the fall — a bonus can still push you into the window with time to adjust.
