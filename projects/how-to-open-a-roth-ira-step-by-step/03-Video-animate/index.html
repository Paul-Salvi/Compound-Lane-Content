What A Roth IRA Does

A Roth IRA is a retirement account where <b>your money grows tax-free and comes out tax-free in retirement</b>. You contribute after-tax dollars (no deduction today), but qualified withdrawals — including earnings — are tax-free. The trade: pay tax now, never pay tax on the growth.

ROTH IN ONE LINE
Pay tax now. Never pay tax on the growth. Qualified withdrawals in retirement are tax-free — including decades of compounding.
2

2026 Contribution Limits

You can contribute up to <b>$7,500 to a Roth IRA in 2026</b>, or $8,600 if you're 50 or older (catch-up). The limit applies across all your IRAs combined — Roth, traditional, SEP. Earned income must equal or exceed what you contribute.

The MAGI Phase-Out

Roth eligibility phases out at higher incomes. <b>Single: $153,000–$168,000 MAGI</b> for 2026. <b>Married filing jointly: $242,000–$252,000</b>. Inside the window your limit shrinks proportionally. Above the top, direct contributions are off — use a backdoor Roth or 401(k).

Step 1: Pick The Brokerage

Open the Roth IRA at a brokerage — Fidelity, Schwab, or Vanguard are the three big ones. The IRA itself is the same at each; the difference is the funds and tools you get. <b>All three offer index funds like FXAIX, VOO, or VTI at the lowest fees</b>. Pick by UI and fund lineup.

TOP THREE

  • Fidelity — FXAIX at 0.015%
  • Schwab — SCHB at 0.03%
  • Vanguard — VOO/VTI at 0.03%

Steps 2-5: Fund And Invest

(2) Open the Roth IRA online in ~10 minutes. (3) Link your bank account. (4) Transfer your contribution ($7,500 max for 2026). (5) Buy a low-cost S&P 500 index fund (FXAIX, VOO) or target date fund for your retirement year. The cash doesn't grow until you invest it.

6

Don't Leave It In Cash

Cash in a Roth IRA earns interest but a fraction of what stocks return. <b>The S&P 500 has averaged ~10% annually since 1928</b> (about 7% after inflation). Investing the money is step 5 for a reason: the IRA wrapper means nothing if the cash sits. Buy VOO, FXAIX, or a target date fund the day you fund it.

DON'T FORGET
Cash in a Roth IRA is safe but slow. Invest it in VOO, FXAIX, or a target date fund. The wrapper is the tax break; the fund is the growth.
