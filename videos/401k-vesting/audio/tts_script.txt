Your 401k match can vanish when you leave — if you're not vested. Your own contributions are always 100% yours. The match may not be. Cliff vesting: zero percent for two years, then 100% at year three. Graded: 20% per year, 100% at year six. Leave at 2.5 years on a cliff? The entire 4500 dollar match is forfeited. On graded at year two — keep only 20%. Thats 3600 dollars lost. A year of service is 1000 hours of work. Check your SPD for your exact schedule. Full math at compoundlane.com.
