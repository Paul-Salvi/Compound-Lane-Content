**2026 legislative state:**
- **OBBBA (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, July 2025) repealed the SECURE 2.0 §603 mandatory-Roth catch-up rule** for high earners earning >$145,000 in prior-year wages. **Effective Jan 1, 2026.** High earners can resume pre-tax catch-up contributions in 401(k)/403(b)/457/TSP. The $145,000 threshold itself is unchanged. (Source: industry summaries of OBBBA §71302; *flag — verify against the public law text before publishing.*)
- **Backdoor Roth and mega backdoor Roth still legal** for 2026. No income limit on conversions.
- **TCJA individual tax rates made permanent by OBBBA** — reducing the "convert before rates go up" urgency some 2025 content pushed.
- **Roth 401(k) RMDs eliminated** for the original owner starting 2024 (SECURE 2.0).
- **No SECURE Act 3.0** enacted. *Do not use that phrase on production content — it's clickbait that won't survive a fact-check.*
