## 1. Locked facts (sourcing gate)

Every number in every reel traces to one of these primary sources. Secondary sources (NerdWallet, Forbes, etc.) cite the IRS primary.

### 2026 limits — IRS Notice 2025-67, IR-2025-111 (Nov 13 2025)

| Item | 2026 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| IRA contribution (under 50) | **$7,500** | $7,000 |
| IRA catch-up (50+) | **$1,100** | $1,000 |
| Total IRA at 50+ | **$8,600** | $8,000 |
| 401(k) elective deferral | **$24,500** | $23,500 |
| 401(k) age 60–63 enhanced catch-up | **$11,250** | $11,250 |
| Roth IRA phase-out — Single | **$153k–$168k** | $150k–$165k |
| Roth IRA phase-out — MFJ | **$242k–$252k** | $236k–$246k |
| Roth IRA phase-out — MFS | **$0–$10k** (no COLA) | $0–$10k |
| HSA self-only | **$4,400** | $4,300 |
| HSA family | **$8,750** | $8,550 |
| SEP IRA max | $70,000 | $69,000 |
| Solo 401(k) §415(c) ceiling | $72,000 | $70,000 |
| SIMPLE IRA elective deferral | $16,500 | $16,000 |

### 2026 rule changes

- **OBBBA (July 2025) repealed SECURE 2.0 §603 mandatory-Roth catch-up** for high earners earning >$145K prior-year wages, effective Jan 1 2026. ⚠️ Verify against OBBBA public law text before publishing any reel on this.
- **TCJA rates made permanent** by OBBBA — reduces "convert before rates rise" urgency.
- **Backdoor + mega backdoor Roth still legal.** No income limit on conversions.
- **Roth 401(k) RMDs eliminated** since 2024 (SECURE 2.0).
- **No "SECURE Act 3.0"** — that phrase is clickbait. Don't use.

### 2026 deadlines that matter in the 30-day window

- **Sept 15, 2026** — Form 1040-ES Q3 estimated tax (NOT a Roth deadline — see Reel #15)
- **Dec 31, 2026** — Roth conversion deadline (calendar-year-end)
- **April 15, 2027** — 2026 Roth contribution deadline

### Penalties & rules (sourced)

- **6% excise tax** on excess IRA contributions — IRC §4973
- **10% early-withdrawal penalty** — IRC §72(t)
- **5-year rule** — IRC §408A(d)(2)(A)
- **59½ rule** — IRC §408A(d)(2)
- **Pro-rata rule** — IRC §408(d)(2); Form 8606
- **Mega backdoor §415(c) ceiling** — $72,000 (2026)
- **10-year rule for inherited IRAs** — SECURE 2.0 §327; IRS TD 10002

### Investment assumptions

- **7% real return** = historical US large-cap equity average (Damodaran, NYU Stern; Vanguard; Morningstar). This is the brand's house rate, used across `dollar-cost-averaging/`, `roth-ira-sp500/`, and this backlog. Stated explicitly in every reel that uses compounding math.
- **15% long-term capital gains rate** = IRC §1(h) (the typical 24% bracket saver's bracket).

## 7. Hook patterns that work (ranked by 2026 finance-creator data)

Cross-referenced from SaliencyLab, Grow Creator, FluxNote, and the YouTube Shorts inventory in `02-trending-formats-and-topics.md`. The pattern matched across all sources:

1. **First 1.9s = scroll-stop decision.** Thumb decision at 0.3–0.8s is visual only. Audio hasn't registered.
2. **Visual stakes on frame one** — face, hand, or result mid-action. Static product shots lose 14 pts.
3. **Spoken promise before brand** — say what the viewer gets in first 0.8s (+28% completion).
4. **Pattern-break audio** at <1s — sub-bass drop, dialect shift, abrupt silence (+6–9 pts).
5. **Caption-first composition** — top-third pinned caption, readable in <600ms.
6. **Forbidden openers** — "hey guys," "today I'm going to," "in this video," "welcome back" = -18 to -22 pts.

### Hook phrasings that recur in the Roth niche (ranked)

1. **"Stop doing X"** — wrong-belief open (creates cognitive dissonance)
2. **"Here's why X is the best deal"** — Compound Lane's signature
3. **"If you're under 30 and not doing X..."** — identity + urgency
4. **"The IRS doesn't want you to know this"** — forbidden knowledge
5. **"I wish someone told me this at 22"** — confession / social proof
6. **"X things nobody tells you about Y"** — listicle
7. **"$X → $Y"** — numerical + transformation
8. **"I made $X mistake"** — confessional + lesson
9. **"The 5-year rule nobody told me about"** — specificity + lost-money
10. **"X vs Y — the math is different than you think"** — comparison + curiosity gap
11. **"POV: your X is your biggest asset at 65"** — POV + visualization
12. **"Three numbers decide X"** — numerical + decision frame

The 30-reel calendar uses a mix of these patterns. Heavy on the "here's why" / "X vs Y" / "three numbers" frames (Compound Lane voice), with storytime + confessional in the mistake lane.

## 8. Production rules (apply to every reel)

Cross-referenced from `AUDIO_STYLE.md`, `docs/pacing-rules-v1.md`, and the existing Compound Lane precedent (DCA, OTDT, Roth reel 1).

### Pacing

- **75–100 word VO** per reel
- **2.3–2.7 wps** (VibeVoice/Paul at 1.20×)
- **Hook ≤ 12 words, lands by 2.0s**
- **7-beat map:** orient → frame → warn → quantify_cost → quantify_opportunity_cost → closing_edge_case → cta
- **Peak (largest) number in segment 3** (quantify_opportunity_cost, 20–30s)
- **Mid-video reset at ~20s** (component change, not a long static frame)
- **CTA in segment 7** (38–40s); single ask only (save / follow / share / comment / "next one")

### Sourcing (every reel)

- **Every number traces** to a source in §1 of this doc or the original IRS publication
- **No "you should" tax advice** — "here's the rule" only
- **No ticker / brokerage / fund recommendation** — provider-agnostic
- **No promised returns** — "the historical real return of US large-cap is ~7%" only
- **2026 limits are IRS Notice 2025-67** — never use a number from a non-IRS source as the lead

### Visual

- **Notebook style** (Caveat + Patrick Hand, paper, ink-navy / ink-maroon / ink-green / ink-yellow, `rough-a/b/c` filters)
- **Num-blob** for the lead number
- **Ledger rows** for the cost / opportunity / rules beats
- **Big-number reveal** for the peak (segment 3)
- **No component-type repeats** within one reel
- **Critical text not in bottom 25% or right edge** (caption-safe zone)

### Voice (Paul / VibeVoice @ 1.20×)

- **Numbers as words** (seven thousand, not 7,000)
- **Em-dash breaths** for pacing
- **No `# section` markers** in `tts_script.txt` (per AUDIO_STYLE rule 8)
- **No per-line commentary** in the script
- **No all-caps**
- **No bullet lists** — continuous prose

### Per-platform copy (01-text/)

- **YouTube:** title (≤60 chars), description (full copy + sources), 5–9 tags
- **Instagram:** hook (≤125 chars), full caption (~300–500 chars, "study notes" format), alt text, 3–5 hashtags
- **Twitter:** single post (70–100 chars, no link in first tweet)
- **YouTube Learning:** 6 timecoded Q&A pairs (Stage 3, post-render)
- **Spanish subs:** SRT for YouTube Studio upload (Stage 4, post-render)
