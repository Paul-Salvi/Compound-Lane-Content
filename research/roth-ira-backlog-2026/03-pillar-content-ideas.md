# Roth IRA 2026 — Pillar Content Ideas

> **Lane:** 30s voiceover reels, notebook-style visuals, IG + YouTube Shorts.
> **Window:** Aug 30 → Sept 29, 2026.
> **Pillar weight in 30-day calendar:** 70% pillar / 20% trend-jacking / 10% mistakes (per brand).
> **Method:** The 70% pillar lane is built on evergreen, high-search-volume Roth IRA topics. This doc lists the 6 pillars, the reel ideas that support each, and the rationale for the mix.

---

## 1. Pillar architecture

A "pillar" is a **topic cluster** that, across 6–12 reels, can credibly cover a subject end-to-end. The brand voice says "every number sourced" and "no jargon unless defined" — the pillars are the lanes that earn the long-tail SEO and the saves.

We define **6 pillars** for the Roth IRA niche. Each pillar maps to the brand's overall taxonomy of `tax mechanics / investing strategy / account types / edge cases / mistakes` (the 5 concept clusters from the production handbook).

| # | Pillar | Concept cluster | What it covers | Reel-budget per 30 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Roth Rules** (limits, phase-outs, deadlines) | tax mechanics + account types | The 2026 contribution limit, MAGI phase-outs, deadlines, catch-ups | 6 reels |
| 2 | **Roth for High Earners** (backdoor + mega backdoor + pro-rata) | investing strategy + edge cases | Strategies for people who can't contribute directly | 5 reels |
| 3 | **Roth vs Everything Else** (Traditional, Roth 401k, HSA, taxable) | account types | Comparison reels | 5 reels |
| 4 | **The Math of Compounding** (Roth as a growth engine) | investing strategy | Compound interest, growth curves, the $7,500 × 30yr anchor | 4 reels |
| 5 | **Mistakes & Penalties** (6% excise, 10% penalty, pro-rata) | mistakes | "Don't do this" reels | 4 reels |
| 6 | **Roth for Real Life** (kids, inheritance, retirement, FIRE) | edge cases | Storytime, procedural, edge case | 6 reels |

> **Total: 30 reels.** That hits ~1 reel per day for 30 days, with 4–5 reels per pillar in the largest clusters. The mix fits the 70/20/10 (pillar / trend-jacking / mistakes) because the trend-jacking is woven into the pillar structure (e.g., "Sept 15 deadline correction" lives inside Pillar 1).

## 2. Pillar 1 — The Roth Rules (6 reels)

**Why this is a pillar:** "Roth IRA contribution limits 2026" and "Roth IRA income limits 2026" are Tier 1 keywords. The 2026 numbers ($7,500 / $8,600; $153k–$168k single; $242k–$252k MFJ) are now confirmed and people are looking them up. This pillar owns the "every number sourced" promise.

| Reel idea | Format | Hook (≤12 words) | Pacing |
|---|---|---|---|
| The 2026 contribution limit just changed | straight explainer | "The 2026 Roth limit is $7,500. Here's what that buys you." | 80 words, big number reveal |
| The $1,100 catch-up at 50 | definition | "Turn 50 this year? You get an extra $1,100 in your Roth." | 75 words |
| Single phase-out ($153k → $168k) | procedural | "Make $153k? Your Roth contribution just started shrinking." | 85 words |
| MFJ phase-out ($242k → $252k) | procedural | "Married filing jointly? The Roth phase-out starts at $242k." | 85 words |
| The deadline you think exists (Sept 15 correction) | mistake + deadline | "The Sept 15 deadline for Roths doesn't exist. Here's the real one." | 90 words |
| April 15 prior-year contributions | procedural | "You can fund a 2025 Roth until April 15, 2026." | 80 words |

**Sourcing gate:** All numbers trace to IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-XX, Forbes Oct 22 2025, Schwab/Vanguard/Fidelity 2026 limits pages, IRS Pub 590-A.

## 3. Pillar 2 — Roth for High Earners (5 reels)

**Why this is a pillar:** "backdoor Roth" + "mega backdoor Roth" + "pro rata rule" are Tier 2 keywords. The $242k MFJ phase-out means **a huge slice of dual-income households can't contribute directly**, so the backdoor is the default plan. This pillar owns the high-earner niche.

| Reel idea | Format | Hook (≤12 words) | Pacing |

| Mega backdoor Roth: $72,000 of Roth in 2026 | procedural | "There's a Roth strategy that moves $72,000 a year. Here's how." | 95 words |
| How to neutralize pro-rata (the 401(k) rollover trick) | procedural | "The pro-rata rule has a workaround. It's a 401(k) rollover." | 85 words |
| Form 8606: the form that does the work | definition | "One IRS form proves your backdoor is tax-free. It's Form 8606." | 85 words |

**Sourcing gate:** All numbers trace to IRS Pub 590-A, IRS Form 8606, Fidelity mega-backdoor page, NerdWallet / White Coat Investor strategy guides, the SmartAsset pro-rata example.

## 8. Series ideas (the brief asked for 2–3)

### Series A — "Roth IRA Myth-Busting" (3 reels)
The brief asks for 3-part myth-busting. Suggested cuts:

1. **"Roths have RMDs. They don't."** — SECURE 2.0 §325 eliminated them in 2024. Common misconception.
2. **"You can contribute to a Roth at any income. You can't."** — phase-out ranges. (Same numbers as Pillar 1.)
3. **"A backdoor Roth avoids all tax. It doesn't, if you have a pre-tax IRA."** — pro-rata rule. (Same numbers as Pillar 2.)

Series framing: title cards "Myth #1 / #2 / #3" or "Roth Myths / Part 1 of 3."

### Series B — "The Math of Compounding" (3 reels)
The brief asks for 3-part math. This is Pillar 4. Suggested cuts:

1. **$7,500/yr for 30 years → $X** (the headline number)
2. **$7,500/yr for 40 years → $X** (the extended number)
3. **Same $7,500 in a taxable brokerage → $X** (the comparison number)

Series framing: title cards "Math of Compounding / Part 1 of 3."

### Series C — "Roth for High Earners" (3 reels)
Bonus series. Suggested cuts:

1. **The backdoor Roth (the simple version)**

Series framing: title cards "Roth for High Earners / Part 1 of 3."

## 10. Production-pacing note

A few reels in this doc are "easier" than others. Pacing:

- **Easy / mostly templated** (low research, low production, "already-sourced"): the big-number reveal reels in Pillar 1; the comparison reels in Pillar 3.
- **Medium** (some new research, scripted properly): the high-earner strategy reels; the inheritance reels.
- **Hard** (need careful sourcing or scenario-building): the pro-rata rule example reel; the cost-of-waiting-5-years reel; the Roth at 22 storytime reel.

The 30-day calendar is paced so that 2 "hard" reels per week max, with the rest medium or easy. This matches Paul / VibeVoice's single-voice, single-day turnaround and the project's standard build cycle.
